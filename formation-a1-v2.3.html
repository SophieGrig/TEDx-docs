Formation A1 v2.3

DATE, what this version explores, branch sophie/formation-studies

Question

Can particles follow a mobile shape?

What was changed?

  • Added heaviness to hands and feet. so more particles are pulled to them
  • Reduced the pull, to make more particles be left behind
  • Slowed down particle movment, to contradict their new chaoticness which resulted from previous changes

What works

  • Particles seem to be following the form consistently
  • Hands and feet now keep their shape in movement
  • Particles behave more organicaly, like they actually notice and are attracted to the form, isntead of just taking its shape

What needs work

Particles arent distributed to the form very evenly. Feature or bug? Remains to be seen.
